Why it mattersImpact

Which polarization shows this?HH · HV

NISAR sends radar waves that vibrate horizontally (H) and listens for echoes in two ways. HH (sent H, received H) is strong from rough ground, buildings and flooded trees. HV (sent H, received V) only comes back when the wave gets scrambled inside a 3-D structure like a tree crown – so it is the best vegetation detector. These images combine both: forests glow because of strong HV, burned slopes lose it, and calm water stays dark in both.
