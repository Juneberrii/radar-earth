Ground Motion

By comparing two radar passes, NISAR can measure ground movement of just a few centimeters – if you know how to separate it from tricks of the sky.

TargetLocked

How to read the rainbow

NISAR compares two passes and measures whether the distance to the ground changed. One full color cycle ≈ 12 cm – half of NISAR's ≈ 24 cm radar wavelength. Empty areas are water or dense vegetation, where the radar can't keep track between dates.
